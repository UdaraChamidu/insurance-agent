1️⃣ Theme color (recommended)



Best choice for insurance (Florida + health):



Primary: Deep blue / navy
Secondary: White / light gray
Accent: Muted green or teal



Why:
• Blue = trust, regulation, professionalism
• Green = health, reassurance
• Avoid aggressive colors (red, orange, neon)



Example palette (you can give this directly):
• Primary: #0B3C5D (navy blue)
• Secondary: #FFFFFF (white)
• Accent: #2E8B57 (soft green)
• Text: #1F2937 (dark gray)



Tell the dev:



Keep it conservative and readable. No gradients, no neon, no dark themes.



⸻



2️⃣ Images to include (very important)



What to include ✅
• Realistic people (families, seniors, professionals)
• Neutral healthcare imagery
• Calm, friendly faces
• Lifestyle shots (not hospitals)



What to avoid ❌
• Robots, AI brains, chat bubbles
• Doctors in scrubs (you’re not a provider)
• Stock photos that look fake or salesy
• Anything that implies medical treatment



Where images go:
• Hero section (one strong image)
• One supporting image mid-page
• Optional small icons (simple line icons only)



Tell the dev:



Use high-quality, neutral stock images. No AI/robot imagery.

4️⃣ Page structure (this is the most important part)



The homepage should have only these sections:



1. Hero section
• Headline
• One sentence explaining who you help
• Primary CTA: “Schedule a Consultation”



2. What we help with



Short bullet list:
• Individual & family health insurance
• Medicare guidance
• Small business coverage
• Life & supplemental insurance



No details. No promises.



3. How it works



3 steps max:
1. Schedule a consultation
2. Discuss your situation with a licensed agent
3. Review available options



4. Why choose us
• Licensed professionals
• Compliance-first approach
• Personalized guidance
• No obligation consultation



5. CTA again



Booking button (Microsoft Bookings)



That’s it. No long scrolling.



⸻



5️⃣ Texts (very important guidance for AI-generated copy)



What AI text is OK for:
• General descriptions
• Process explanations
• Friendly tone
• Marketing language



What MUST be reviewed/edited manually:
• Anything that sounds like:
• “We recommend…”
• “You qualify for…”
• “Best plan for you”
• “Guaranteed savings”
• Anything that sounds like advice or decisions



Tell the dev:



Generate draft text if needed, but I will review and approve all copy. Avoid definitive or advisory language.



Safe wording examples:
• “We help you understand your options”
• “We guide you through available choices”
• “We explain how coverage works”



Unsafe wording to avoid:
• “We choose the best plan for you”
• “We ensure you qualify”
• “AI-powered recommendations”
