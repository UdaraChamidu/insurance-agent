Teams vs Twilio (for your use case)



Microsoft Teams
• ✅ Great for video meetings + screen share
• ✅ Easy for internal staff
• ✅ Already in M365
• ⚠️ Harder to build a reliable “agent assist” call flow:
• Getting real-time audio/transcripts + speaker labels + event hooks is more constrained
• Recording/export + linking to CRM + audit trail is less programmable
• You end up depending on Microsoft’s meeting experience rather than your own app



Twilio (WebRTC softphone in your private agent app)
• ✅ Built for programmable telephony
• ✅ Real-time audio streaming → transcription → AI response is straightforward
• ✅ You control:
• recording on/off
• retention links
• call events (start/hold/transfer/end)
• logging (disposition, audit fields)
• ✅ Integrates cleanly with your backend + CRM
• ✅ The private agent app becomes the “system of record”
• ⚠️ More engineering + costs than “just use Teams”



What I recommend



Use both, but for different jobs:
• Teams/Zoom = optional screen share / video meetings
• Twilio = primary phone call + AI assist + logging + compliance record



If you only choose one for everything:
• Choose Twilio if the goal is: AI assistant during calls + full audit trail + automation
• Choose Teams if the goal is: simple meetings only (minimal AI)
