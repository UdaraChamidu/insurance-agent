================================================================================
  SECTION 3: CLIENT'S MINIMUM VIABLE WORKFLOW (10-Step Lead-to-Renewal)
================================================================================

The client defined this exact workflow for group health insurance:

  Step  1: Lead comes in (website form, referral, cold outreach)
  Step  2: Initial contact (phone call, email, or text)
  Step  3: Discovery call (understand needs, company size, current coverage)
  Step  4: Collect census data (employee demographics for quoting)
  Step  5: Submit to Warner Pacific (for carrier quotes)
  Step  6: Receive quotes from Warner Pacific
  Step  7: Present proposal to employer (compare options, recommendations)
  Step  8: Close the deal (enrollment, paperwork)
  Step  9: Onboard (submit forms, set up coverage)
  Step 10: Renewal management (track renewal dates, annual re-quoting)

STATUS: The 10-step workflow maps directly to the 11-stage group CRM pipeline
        (new_lead -> contacted -> discovery_scheduled -> census_requested ->
        census_received -> sent_to_warner -> quotes_received ->
        proposal_presented -> closed_won / closed_lost -> renewal_followup).
        All stages are implemented and functional.
