Public Website (Squarespace) – What the customer sees



A. Landing page (high-level, trust-first)



They should see:
• Who you are (Elite Deal Broker)
• What you help with (health insurance guidance)
• Who it’s for (individuals, families, small businesses)
• Why you’re different (clarity, compliance, human guidance)
• Clear call to action: “Schedule a consultation”



They should NOT see:
• “AI-powered”
• “Chatbot”
• “Automated decisions”
• Anything that sounds like advice without a human



📌 This protects you legally and builds trust.



⸻



B. Before booking – light intake (very important)



This is NOT underwriting.
This is context gathering.



Recommended questions (safe + useful):
1. What type of insurance are you looking for?
• Individual / Family
• Medicare
• Small business
• ACA marketplace
• Not sure yet
2. What state are you currently in?
• Dropdown (US states)
3. Have you recently experienced a life event? (optional)
• Moved to a new state
• Lost coverage
• Turned 65
• Employer coverage change
• None
4. Preferred consultation type
• Phone
• Video
5. Anything you want us to know before the call?
• Free text (optional)



Why this is perfect:
• No advice
• No eligibility decisions
• No promises
• Fully compliant
• Extremely helpful for AI prep later



⸻



C. Booking confirmation page



They should see:
• Date & time
• Who they’ll speak with
• What will happen on the call
• What to prepare (ID, current plan, questions)



Add a small disclaimer (important):



“This consultation is informational and does not constitute legal or financial advice. Coverage eligibility and pricing are determined by carriers and regulators.”
